Curriculum revision and requirements review

The revised 130-credit plan makes Data Engineering and Cloud Computing & DevOps compulsory while retaining specialist choice. This review records planned curriculum coverage and progression; accreditation also depends on approved policies, resources, delivered teaching, assessment and attainment evidence.

Course sequence update CS 330 Theory of Computation is in Semester 5. CS 350 Human–Computer Interaction is in Semester 7 and may be taken concurrently with Capstone I. Semester 5 Web and Mobile Development retains introductory interface-design and accessibility teaching. Credit totals and mandatory coverage remain unchanged.

Compulsory coverage retained when courses become electives

CS 350 includes a defined graphics strand and an individual practical. CS 340 includes introductory ML, evaluation, subgroup analysis and AI ethics. CS 451 supplies required program-assessment evidence for K3 and S4. CS 103 owns graph isomorphism and mathematical spanning-tree evidence; CS 201 retains implementation and CS 220 develops advanced algorithms.

Elective choices and prerequisites

Choose three different options. MLOps plus Machine Learning uses two elective slots; the third remains available. Cloud Computing & DevOps is compulsory and can be concurrent with MLOps. Other new electives have required-course prerequisites completed before Semester 8.
