Applied learning

From a workplace assignment to a capstone

CS 390 · 9 credits

One full-time cooperative placement

Assessment combines supervisor appraisal, progress logs, an evidence portfolio, and a college presentation.

CS 410 + CS 411 · 6 credits

Two consecutive capstone courses

In Semester 7, teams define a problem, apply techniques from earlier courses, and test a prototype. In Semester 8, they integrate, evaluate, and defend the completed solution.

Each student documents their own contribution and explains the project’s professional and technical decisions.

Example capstone · Appointment coordination

Use synthetic appointment records to design a scheduling service for a clinic. In Capstone I, interview representative users, model constraints, and prototype the workflow. In Capstone II, test the service against a baseline and report wait times, scheduling conflicts, accessibility, and data-handling decisions.

ETEC essential and general knowledge units

ETEC 2025 version 2.1 requires 30 credits in mathematics, statistics and basic sciences. The plan allocates 20 to mathematics and statistics, 6 to physics, and 4 to biology. Computing Project Management and Finance has a separate three-credit allocation. The required CS core totals 61 credits.

The current 130-credit plan requires CS 451 Data Engineering and CS 455 Cloud Computing & DevOps in Semester 8. CS 201 Data Structures is three credits with two practical hours weekly and coordinated coverage in CS 103 and CS 220. Machine Learning and Graphics are three-credit electives. All students retain assessed ML foundations and AI ethics in CS 340 and graphics foundations in CS 350. Semester totals are 17, 16, 16, 18, 18, 9, 18 and 18.

Courses in the map below account for 43 distinct required CS core credits. CS 103 contributes three of the 20 mathematics/statistics credits within the 30-credit Essential Knowledge Units allocation; its CS prefix does not add it to the 61-credit CS core.
