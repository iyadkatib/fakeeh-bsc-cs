Applied learning

From a workplace assignment to a capstone

CS 390 · 9 credits

One full-time cooperative placement

Assessment combines supervisor appraisal, progress logs, an evidence portfolio, and a college presentation.

CS 410 + CS 411 · 6 credits

Two consecutive capstone courses

In Semester 7, teams define a problem, apply techniques from earlier courses, and test a prototype. In Semester 8, they integrate, evaluate, and defend the completed solution.

Each student documents their own contribution and explains the project’s professional and technical decisions.

Example capstone · Appointment coordination

Use synthetic appointment records to design a scheduling service for a clinic. In Capstone I, interview representative users, model constraints, and prototype the workflow. In Capstone II, test the service against a baseline and report wait times, scheduling conflicts, accessibility, and data-handling decisions.

ETEC essential and general knowledge units

Essential Knowledge Units total 30 credits with 17 in mathematics and statistics, 6 in physics, 4 in chemistry, and 3 in project management and finance. Mathematics, physics, and chemistry reach 23 credits even when the combined probability and statistics course is excluded from that subtotal. The 67 required CS core credits are counted separately.

The shared ethics course increases to three credits. Multivariable Calculus and Optimization moves to three credits and retains the gradient and optimization topics needed for Machine Learning. Using the 15-credit ABET mathematics minimum with the current science and project management credits would yield 28 Essential Knowledge Unit credits.

Courses in the map below account for 51 distinct required CS core credits. Mathematics courses support the discrete structures topics and are counted in the Essential Knowledge Units.
